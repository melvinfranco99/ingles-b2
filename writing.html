Writing

80 minutos, 2 textos de 140–190 palabras. Parte 1: essay obligatorio. Parte 2: eliges uno entre tres (article, email/letter, review, report). Es la prueba que más se puede mejorar con preparación: si memorizas estructuras y frases, tienes media nota asegurada.

Gestión del tiempo (40 min por texto)

  1. 5 min · Planificar: subraya qué te piden, decide los párrafos y una idea por párrafo.
  2. 30 min · Escribir siguiendo tu plan.
  3. 5 min · Revisar: concordancia (he goes), tiempos verbales, ortografía, plurales, artículos, que no falte ningún punto.

Essay (Parte 1, obligatorio)

Situación: «In your English class you have been talking about… Now your teacher has asked you to write an essay.» Te dan una pregunta y dos notas; tú añades una tercera idea propia. Registro: formal/neutro (sin contracciones, sin coloquialismos).

Estructura (4–5 párrafos)

  1. Introducción (2–3 frases): presenta el tema con tus palabras (no copies la pregunta) y anuncia que vas a considerar varios aspectos.
  2. Nota 1 + razón + ejemplo.
  3. Nota 2 + razón + ejemplo (puede ser la visión contraria).
  4. Tu idea (la tercera, distinta de las dos).
  5. Conclusión: tu opinión clara, resumiendo. Sin ideas nuevas.
Tarea modelo

Some people say that young people should do unpaid work in their community. Do you agree?
Notes: 1. skills · 2. time for studies · 3. ……… (your own idea)

Volunteering has become increasingly common among teenagers and young adults. But should every young person be expected to work for free in their local community? In my view, there are several factors to take into account.

[Nota 1] Firstly, unpaid work can help young people develop skills that schools rarely teach. For instance, helping at a food bank or a sports club teaches teamwork, responsibility and how to deal with the public, all of which are highly valued by employers.

[Nota 2] On the other hand, students already have a heavy workload. If they were forced to volunteer, they might have less time to study and rest, which could affect their results and even their health.

[Idea propia] Another point worth considering is the benefit to society. Elderly people, for example, often feel isolated, and a weekly visit from a volunteer can make a real difference to their lives.

All things considered, I believe that young people should be encouraged, rather than obliged, to do community work. Provided that it does not interfere with their studies, it is a valuable experience for both the volunteers and the people they help.

≈ 190 palabras · conectores: Firstly, For instance, On the other hand, Another point, All things considered · estructuras: segundo condicional, relativo «all of which», provided that.

Frases útiles

  • Introducir: It is often said that… / Nowadays, more and more people… / This is a controversial issue…/ There are several factors to take into account.
  • Argumentar: One of the main advantages of… is that… / Another point worth considering is… / It could be argued that… / There is no doubt that…
  • Contrastar: On the other hand, … / However, … / Nevertheless, … / Despite this, …
  • Ejemplos: For instance, … / A good example of this is… / such as…
  • Concluir: All things considered, … / On balance, I believe… / To sum up, … / In conclusion, it seems clear that…

Article

Situación: una revista, web o blog pide artículos sobre un tema (Tell us about a place that is special to you…). Objetivo: interesar y entretener al lector. Registro neutro-informal, personal, con ejemplos y anécdotas. Se permiten contracciones.

Estructura

  1. Título atractivo (obligatorio en la práctica).
  2. Introducción que enganche: pregunta retórica, dato sorprendente o situación con la que el lector se identifique.
  3. 2–3 párrafos de desarrollo, respondiendo a todas las preguntas de la tarea.
  4. Conclusión con un comentario final, una recomendación o una pregunta al lector.
Tarea modelo

Articles wanted! A day without technology. Could you spend a whole day without your phone, computer or TV? What would you do? Would it be easy or difficult? Write us an article answering these questions.

Unplugged: could you survive?

Be honest: how many times have you checked your phone today? Ten? Twenty? For most of us, the idea of a whole day without screens sounds almost impossible. But last month, I decided to try it.

The first few hours were the hardest. I kept reaching for my phone without thinking, and I felt strangely anxious about the messages I might be missing. By lunchtime, however, something had changed. I went for a long walk in the park, and for the first time in ages I actually noticed what was around me: the birds, the people, even the smell of the trees.

In the afternoon, I dug out an old novel I had never finished and read it from cover to cover. In the evening, instead of watching a series, I cooked dinner with my flatmates and we talked for hours.

So, was it easy? Not at all. But was it worth it? Absolutely. I'm not planning to throw away my phone, but I've promised myself one screen-free day a month. Why don't you give it a go?

Título · pregunta retórica al inicio · anécdota personal · vocabulario expresivo (dig out, from cover to cover, give it a go) · pregunta final al lector.

Frases útiles

  • Have you ever wondered…? / Imagine… / Picture the scene: …/ Let’s face it, …
  • To be honest, … / Believe it or not, … / What I love most about… is… / The best thing about… is…
  • So, what are you waiting for? / Why not give it a try? / If you ever get the chance, don’t miss it!

I am a 21-year-old university student and I have a great deal of experience working with the public. Tarea modelo (informal)

Your English friend Sam writes: «I'm coming to Spain for a week in April! What's the best city to visit? What should I do there? And what's the weather like at that time?»

Hi Sam,

That's brilliant news! I'm so glad you're finally coming to Spain – you're going to love it.

If I were you, I'd definitely go to Seville. April is the perfect time because the orange trees are in blossom and the whole city smells amazing. Plus, there's the Feria, a huge festival with music, dancing and traditional dresses. It's a bit crowded, but it's an experience you'll never forget.

As for things to do, make sure you visit the Alcázar – it's a stunning palace with incredible gardens. You should also try the tapas in the Triana neighbourhood. Don't leave without trying salmorejo, a cold tomato soup that's absolutely delicious!

The weather is usually lovely in April, around 22 or 23 degrees, but it can rain now and then, so pack a light jacket just in case.

Let me know your dates and I'll try to come down and show you around!

Take care,
Pablo

Review

Una reseña de película, libro, serie, restaurante, app, hotel, museo… para una revista o web. Debe describir, dar tu opinión y terminar con una recomendación. Registro neutro, vivo, con adjetivos variados.

Estructura

  1. Título (el nombre de lo que reseñas).
  2. Introducción: qué es, dónde/cuándo, contexto.
  3. Descripción: argumento (sin destripar el final), características…
  4. Valoración: lo positivo y lo negativo.
  5. Recomendación: a quién se lo recomiendas y por qué.
Tarea modelo

Reviews wanted: a film that made you think. Write a review of a film that made you think about an important issue. Say what the film is about, what you learnt, and whether you would recommend it.

Wall-E: a small robot with a big message

At first glance, Wall-E looks like a simple animated film for children. However, behind its cute main character lies a powerful story about the future of our planet.

The film is set hundreds of years from now, when Earth has been abandoned by humans because it is covered in rubbish. Wall-E, a lonely little robot, spends his days cleaning up the mess until he meets EVE, a robot sent to look for signs of life. What follows is a touching adventure that takes them into space.

What impressed me most was how much the film says with so few words. There is hardly any dialogue in the first half hour, yet the images of mountains of waste made me think seriously about how much we consume and throw away. The animation is stunning, and the soundtrack is beautiful. If I had to criticise something, I would say the ending is slightly predictable.

Overall, I would highly recommend Wall-E to viewers of all ages. It is entertaining, moving and, above all, a reminder that we only have one planet.

Report

Un informe para un superior (profesor, jefe, director de un club) que analiza una situación y hace recomendaciones. Registro formal e impersonal. Es el formato más fácil de organizar porque usa títulos.

Estructura

  1. To / From / Subject (opcional pero recomendable) o un título.
  2. Introduction: The aim/purpose of this report is to…
  3. 2–3 secciones con subtítulos según lo que pida la tarea.
  4. Recommendations / Conclusion: I would recommend… / It would be a good idea to…
Tarea modelo

The director of your college wants to improve the facilities for students. Write a report describing the current facilities, saying which ones need improving and recommending how the money should be spent.

Report on student facilities

Introduction
The aim of this report is to evaluate the facilities currently available to students and to suggest how the college budget could be used to improve them. The information is based on a survey of 120 students.

Current situation
Most students are satisfied with the library, which is well equipped and open until late. The sports hall is also popular, although some of the equipment is rather old.

Areas for improvement
The main complaint concerns the canteen. It is often overcrowded at lunchtime, and many students feel that the food is overpriced and not very healthy. In addition, a large number of students mentioned that there are not enough quiet places to study in groups.

Recommendations
In view of the above, I would recommend that the majority of the money be spent on renovating the canteen, offering healthier and more affordable meals. It would also be a good idea to convert one of the unused classrooms into a group study area. If these changes were made, students' satisfaction would undoubtedly increase.

Frases útiles

Errores frecuentes de hispanohablantes

  • ❌ People is → ✅ People are. ❌ The people think (generalizando) → ✅ People think.
  • ❌ It’s important study → ✅ It’s important to study. ❌ Is a good idea → ✅ It is a good idea (¡el sujeto es obligatorio!).
  • ❌ I am agree → ✅ I agree. ❌ Depend of → ✅ depend on.
  • ❌ Informations, advices, furnitures → incontables, sin -s.
  • ❌ Since three years → ✅ for three years.
  • ❌ I have 20 years → ✅ I am 20. ❌ Make a photo → ✅ take a photo.
  • ❌ Explain me → ✅ explain to me. ❌ Discuss about → ✅ discuss something.
  • ❌ Actually (= actualmente) → ✅ nowadays / currently.
  • ❌ The life is short → ✅ Life is short.
